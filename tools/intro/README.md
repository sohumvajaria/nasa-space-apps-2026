# Intro tutorial (work in progress, storyboard stage)

A 30-second "Pin Wall" style intro that teaches the Earth Jukebox: home pulse, walking, rain (louder and brighter),
the extreme tremble, the Earth Orchestra (key 6) and then & now (key T).

- `style.js`: the scene (pins, light, camera, captions) and its sound score. Needs `lib/stage.js`, `lib/sound.js`
  (rendering and sound kits, unchanged) and `src/geo.js` (Natural Earth outlines, used for the coastlines).
- `render.html`: renders stills for the storyboard (expects three.js 0.187.0 and the Oswald/Inter fonts locally).

Not wired into the app yet: waiting for the team to approve the storyboard.
