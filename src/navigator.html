What the sounds mean, and the keys
SoundMeans
Steady tone (pitch)The data value where you are. Higher means more: warmer water, heavier rain, hotter ground, denser plants or thicker haze.
Silence and a soft thudLand or open water where this layer has no data
ChimeYou crossed the equator (rising) or the prime meridian (falling)
Metronome speedHow far the target is: faster means closer
Metronome directionWhich way the target is (use headphones). Higher ticks mean north, lower mean south.
Rising chordYou arrived at the target
