How to listen

Use headphones. Move with ←↑↓→ (or WASD). You always face up (north). The sound is at the center (0, 0): it gets quieter with distance and pans, filters and delays to show which direction it is in. Pick a layer above (rain, land heat, plants, ocean heat or haze). Each layer adds its own second note to the center note, and the more intense the data is where you stand, the louder and brighter that note gets: faint at the low end, clear in the middle, full and harsh at the top. Press H to hear a short spoken summary, use the sound key to preview each level. Switch layers with 1 to 5 (rain, land heat, plants, ocean heat, haze), and sounds with Z (synth) and X (soft pulse). V switches between the 3D terrain and the flat map. Hold Shift to move faster, M to mute.
