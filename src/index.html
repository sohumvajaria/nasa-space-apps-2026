Earth Jukebox: spatial audio prototype

Position: 0, 0 Distance: 0.0 Volume: 100% Bearing: -
Use headphones. Move with ←↑↓→ (or WASD). You always face up (north). The sound is at the center (0, 0): it gets quieter with distance and pans, filters and delays to show which direction it is in. Hold Shift to move faster, M to mute.
