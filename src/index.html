Use headphones. Move with ←↑↓→ (or WASD). You always face up (north). The sound is at the center (0, 0): it gets quieter with distance and pans, filters and delays to show which direction it is in. The map shows NASA precipitation: the heavier the rain you stand in, the louder and brighter the minor-sixth note above the center note. Green is a soft hum, yellow is clear, orange is strong, red is full and harsh. Hold Shift to move faster, M to mute.
