How to listen
  • Use headphones. North is always straight ahead.
  • ↑↓←→ or WASD Move. Hold Shift to go faster.
  • The steady beat comes from Null Island (0, 0). Follow it to get back to the center.
  • Violin is rain, cello is land heat, flute is plants, clarinet is ocean heat, and the breeze is wind. Louder means stronger.
  • A shaking sound means extreme data.
  • 1 to 5 Change layer. 6 Earth Orchestra (all layers).
  • H Where you are. L Current layer.
  • , One day back. . One day forward. Add Shift for a week.
  • T Go back to 2006. Press again to return.
  • 7 8 9 0 Sound key, from weakest to strongest.
  • V 3D or flat map. M Mute. G Guide. I Replay intro.
  • N Voice. Choose "screen reader" if you use one.
  • B Vibration on or off (phones).
